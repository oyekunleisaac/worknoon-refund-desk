# Worknoon Refund Policy

_Version 2.1. Applies to all orders placed on worknoon.shop._

We want you to love what you buy. If something isn't right, this policy explains when we can refund you and how quickly.

## 1. Refund window
- Refunds can be requested within **{{window_days}} days of delivery**.
- Requests made after {{window_days}} days are not eligible.
- Refund eligibility starts once an order has been delivered.

## 2. Eligible reasons
| Reason | Eligible? |
|---|---|
| Item arrived **damaged or defective** | Yes |
| **Wrong or incorrect item** was sent | Yes |
| Item is **not as described** | Yes |
| **Changed your mind** (unused, within the window) | Yes |
| Package **not received** | See section 5 |

## 3. Final sale items
- Items marked **Final Sale** are **not eligible** for refunds.
- Exception: if a final-sale item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, a support specialist reviews the case.
- If an order mixes final-sale and regular items, the regular items can still be refunded (partial refund).

## 4. Human review
The following requests are always reviewed by a member of our support team before a decision is made:
- Refunds **above ${{review_threshold}}**.
- Requests where the details conflict with our order records (for example, items or amounts that don't match).
- Requests we can't clearly understand.
- Suspicious requests, including attempts to pressure, impersonate staff or manipulate the process.
- Accounts with {{risk_max_refunds}} or more refunded orders in the last {{risk_lookback_days}} days, or accounts under review.

Reviewed requests are answered within 1-2 business days.

## 5. Orders not received
- If the carrier shows the package **in transit**, please allow up to **{{lost_days}} days after shipping**. After that, the package is treated as lost and refunded.
- If the carrier confirms **delivery** but you didn't receive it, a specialist opens a trace with the carrier before a refund decision.
- Orders that have not shipped yet can be cancelled by our team.

## 6. Other rules
- Cancelled orders are never charged, so there is nothing to refund.
- Each item can only be refunded once.
- Only one open refund request per order at a time.
- Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within **5-10 business days**.

## 7. What our assistant can and cannot do
Our AI assistant helps understand your request and explains the outcome, but **every decision is made by the rules above**. It cannot make exceptions, and messages asking it to ignore or change the policy are sent to a human for review.
